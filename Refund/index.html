Last updated: September 2026.

Cancellation by you

  • Cancel more than 48 hours before your batch or cohort starts: full refund.

If a batch or cohort is cancelled or rescheduled by Wellmma and the new dates do not suit you, you will receive a full refund of the fee paid.
