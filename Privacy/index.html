Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026.

What we collect

  • Parent name and contact details (phone/WhatsApp number) provided at registration.
  • Child's first name and age range, used only to tailor program activities.
  • Payment confirmations from Razorpay (we never see or store your card or bank details).
  • Program progress information you choose to share during reporting.

How we use it

  • To confirm registrations and reserve seats.
  • To run the programs, including live sessions and family reporting.
  • To contact you about your batch, schedule changes and support.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data or your child's data.
  • We never show advertising to children.
  • We never share your information with third parties except payment processing (Razorpay) and essential communication tools.

Children's privacy

Wellmma Playground is parent-controlled. All accounts and registrations are created by parents. Children participate only under parent supervision, and we collect the minimum information needed to run the program.

Data retention and your rights

We keep registration records only as long as needed to run the programs and meet legal obligations. You may ask us at any time to correct or delete your information by contacting +91 94894 65025 on WhatsApp.

Questions? Reach us on WhatsApp at +91 94894 65025 or through our WhatsApp channel.
