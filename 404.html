Challenge structure

Week by week, this is exactly what happens.

  1. Day 1

    Understand where you are

    A baseline snapshot of your habits, health and mindset — no fixing yet, only seeing clearly.

    • An honest starting point
    • Baseline scores
    • No fixing yet — only seeing
  2. Week 1

    Awareness & Observation

    You track everything without judgement so the pattern behind your week becomes visible.

    • Current habits
    • Current routines
    • Behaviour patterns
    • Physical wellbeing
    • Mental wellbeing
    • Emotional wellbeing
  3. Week 2 LIVE SESSION

    Analysis & Understanding

    A full personal and SWOT analysis — what's working, what's costing you, and what triggers repeat.

    • Personal analysis
    • Habit analysis
    • SWOT analysis
    • Identify problems
    • Identify triggers
    • Understand what needs to change
  4. Week 3 LIVE SESSION

    Replacement & Action

    Old habits get replaced with designed behaviour, turned into todos and routines you actually run.

    • Replace unhealthy habits
    • Build better habits
    • Create actionable todos
    • Create routines
    • Make better decisions
    • Start implementing changes
  5. Week 4

    Future & Continuation

    You review 30 days of evidence and turn it into a roadmap for what comes next.

    • Review progress
    • Identify improvements
    • Identify remaining problems
    • Create a future roadmap
    • Decide what to continue
    • Decide what to change next
    • Build a sustainable routine
  6. Final stage

    Transformation & Review

    Keep what worked, drop what didn't, and design the next chapter with proof behind every decision.

    • Look back at 30 days of evidence
    • Keep what worked
    • Design the next chapter

ReHabit is not about becoming perfect.

What is ReHabit

Three dimensions. One connected system.

Wellmma: Re-Habit is built around the idea that wellbeing is not one thing. It is three things that constantly influence each other.

Physical health

Sleep, water, movement, nutrition and routine — the base layer everything else stands on.

Attention, reflection, planning and self-awareness — how clearly you see your own patterns.
